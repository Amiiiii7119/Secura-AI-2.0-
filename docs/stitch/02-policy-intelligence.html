Legal Authority Notice

The points below reflect statutory provisions under IT Rules 2021 (Rule 3(1)(b)) and MeitY Advisory dated March 15, 2024. Secura AI's automated blocking of undisclosed synthetic media is an autonomous platform policy enforcing these regulatory requirements.

1

Statutory & Regulatory Obligations (IT Rules 2021 & Advisories)

Under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, intermediaries hold a positive obligation to not knowingly host, upload, or share content that deceives or misleads the recipient about the origin or nature of such messages, particularly deepfakes and synthetically fabricated representations of natural persons.

2

Mandatory Labeling & Watermarking Requirements

The March 2024 MeitY directive mandates that any intermediary permitting synthetically generated or modified audio-visual materials must deploy prominent visual watermarks or permanent cryptographically verifiable metadata markers. Content generated through generative models must unambiguously signal computer alteration at the presentation layer.

Section 4: Synthetic Audio-Visual Directives Matched [MeitY-ADV-2024-03 §4]
// Official Gazette Reproduction - Paras 4(i) through 4(iii)

4(i) All intermediaries operating within the sovereign digital perimeter shall exercise immediate due diligence with reference to synthetic, deep-fake, and simulated human identities.

"...intermediaries shall ensure that computer resources do not host or permit hosting of synthetically created or altered audio-visual content without prominent label or metadata embedded to identify that such content has been created using AI or computer software..."

4(iii) Failure to implement automated cryptographic tagging or user-facing notices within 72 hours of publication will attract revocation of immunity granted under Sub-Section (1) of Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
